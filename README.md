# Renn Shopify theme

Online Store 2.0 theme for Renn: scroll-driven product film, four product cards, routine, ingredients, reviews, FAQ and email signup.

## Connect to Shopify
1. Shopify admin: **Online Store > Themes > Add theme > Connect from GitHub**.
2. Pick the `marketing` repo and the `renn-shopify-theme` branch.
3. Upload `renn-film.mp4` in **Content > Files**, then in **Customize > Scroll film** pick it as the film.
4. Pick your four products in the "Product pour" blocks and the Routine picker answers.

## Edit
- Prices, photos, product names: in **Products** (the theme reads them from Shopify).
- Text: in **Customize**, click any section.
- Social links and contact email: **Theme settings > Renn**.
