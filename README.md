# Renn Shopify theme

Online Store 2.0 theme for Renn: scroll-driven product film (a 242-frame sequence in assets/seq-*.jpg, nothing to upload, works on phones), four product cards, routine, ingredients, reviews, FAQ and email signup.

## Connect to Shopify
1. Shopify admin: **Online Store > Themes > Add theme > Connect from GitHub**.
2. Pick the `marketing` repo and the `renn-shopify-theme` branch.
3. Pick your four products in the "Product pour" blocks and the Routine picker answers.

## Edit
- Prices, photos, product names: in **Products** (the theme reads them from Shopify).
- Text: in **Customize**, click any section.
- Social links and contact email: **Theme settings > Renn**.
